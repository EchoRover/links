Real: every footprint outline and every interior wall in M1–M4, taken from the ground-floor CAD embedded in the public campus PDF. Assumed: storey counts, and therefore every height. That drawing is a single level and says nothing about how tall anything is, so heights are a guess except for M2, M3 and M4 — a ~ on a label means the height is guessed. M3 and M4 are two storeys because the fire-evacuation boards inside them show Ground and Level 01; M2 has at least three because the timetable books a room on level 2. Incomplete: the site drawing's massing layer does not cover the whole campus, so the library, convention centre, dining halls and some residences are missing entirely.
