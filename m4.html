Where the rooms come from. The two fire-evacuation boards inside M4, segmented by colour. The boards are the source of truth for layout because the CAD has stray linework inside the big rooms that keeps splitting them. What is still rough. Scale is a bounding-box fit onto M4's CAD footprint, not yet a proper four-corner registration, so every dimension carries a few percent of the photograph's perspective. Sanity check: the Lectures Hall comes out 131 m². Not in here yet. Doors, stairs, the corridor and the top-centre service rooms on the ground floor, and the Library and conference rooms on the first floor — the detector missed those and they get added by hand in the plan editor.
