Timetable

Sources. Mon–Thu comes from the printed shift poster, transcribed 19 Aug 2026. Friday is that same poster minus the campus notice of 22 Aug: “there will be no trips between Campus and Dorms from 12:30 PM to 1:15 PM for friday.” The three cancelled runs are struck through rather than removed, so the trip numbers still match the printed sheet; whether the 1:15 PM run itself departs is not stated, and this page assumes it does. Sat & Sun comes from the campus notice of 22 Aug 2026: “Saturdays and Sundays, vehicles will operate between the campus and KCA 1,2 & 3 every 30 minutes in both directions, starting from 7:30 AM.” Countdowns use your device clock, so they only line up if your phone is on Gulf time. Known gaps. The weekend notice gives no last departure and no vehicle rota, so the weekend table has no Vehicle column and its post-7 PM runs sit in their own dashed block marked assumed — they are this page’s guess, not the notice. Neither source says anything about public holidays. Trips 16 and 17 on the Campus → Dorms weekday leg are both printed as 10:00 AM; that is reproduced as printed, not corrected.
